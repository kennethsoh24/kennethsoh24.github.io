金継ぎKINTSUGI
FILE 0610-B · POST-EVENT RECORD漆・炭化

Joined in gold.
Undone by heat.

In kintsugi, a broken vessel is not thrown away. Its pieces are bonded with urushi, the sap of the lacquer tree, and the seams are dusted with powdered gold. Mended this way, a bowl can outlive the hands that made it, and the break becomes the most precious part.

But the gold is only a surface. What holds the pieces together is the lacquer underneath, and lacquer has limits. Keep it cool and the seam can last for generations. In this work, 100°C is the line: past it the urushi scorches and chars to carbon. The gold still shines, but nothing is holding it any more.

What is mended with gold can be eternal, but only under the right conditions. Past them, the same repair becomes a fragility you can't come back from. Cooling it down afterwards does not undo the burn.

THE HEART課題の分離

This is not only about a bowl. It is about a heart that has been broken and mended too many times. Every repair holds, but only as long as the heat stays low.

The heat is everything that was never yours to carry: other people's choices, their moods, their expectations of you. The psychologist Alfred Adler called the answer the separation of tasks. Ask whose task this really is, then carry your own and hand the rest back.

Without that line, the heat rises one beat at a time. A heart can be mended in gold again and again. But if it never learns where it ends and others begin, one day the seams stop holding, and it bursts.
